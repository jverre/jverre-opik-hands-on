# Accessibility at the Alderbank Museum

## Step-free access
The main entrance on Quay Street is step-free, and so are the Chronometer Gallery, the
Tidal Hall, the cafe and the Children's Workshop. A passenger lift in the East Wing serves
all three floors, so the Orrery Room on the second floor can be reached without stairs.
The Lantern Cellar has no lift and is reachable only by a spiral stair.

## Wheelchairs and mobility aids
Manual wheelchairs can be borrowed free of charge from the cloakroom, which is next to the
main entrance, and cannot be booked in advance. Mobility scooters may be brought in and
are welcome at no charge. Folding stools are available at every gallery entrance.

## Quiet visits
On the second Saturday of each month the museum runs a quiet morning from 10:00 to 12:00.
Sound effects are switched off, the lighting is raised in the Tidal Hall, and the cafe has
a low-noise corner. Ear defenders and sensory backpacks can be borrowed from the cloakroom
at any time.

## Assistance dogs and carers
Assistance dogs are welcome in all galleries, including the Lantern Cellar, and water bowls
are kept at the cloakroom and the cafe. A registered carer enters free of charge when
accompanying a visitor with a disability.

## Large print and audio
Large-print gallery guides are free at the main desk, and every gallery label has a QR code
linking to an audio description. A British Sign Language tour of the Tidal Hall runs on the
third Thursday of each month at 11:30 and does not need to be booked.
