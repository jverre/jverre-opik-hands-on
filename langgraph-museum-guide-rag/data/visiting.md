# Visiting the Alderbank Museum

## Opening hours
The museum is open Tuesday to Sunday from 10:00 to 17:30 and is closed on Mondays, except
for public holiday Mondays, when it opens from 11:00 to 16:00. The last entry is at 16:45.
The museum is closed on 25 and 26 December and on 1 January. The Orrery Room closes thirty
minutes earlier than the rest of the museum, at 17:00.

## Tickets and prices
Adult tickets cost 14 pounds and concessions cost 9 pounds. Children under 12 enter free
with a paying adult, and each adult may bring up to three children. A Family Pass for two
adults and up to four children costs 36 pounds. Tickets bought online are 1 pound cheaper
than tickets bought at the desk.

## Free entry
Entry is free for everyone on the first Sunday of each month, and free at all times for
registered carers accompanying a visitor with a disability, and for members of the
Alderbank Friends scheme. Free-entry Sundays are busiest between 12:00 and 15:00, and
timed tickets are not required.

## Getting here
The museum stands on Quay Street, a five-minute walk from Alderbank harbour station. Buses
12 and 40 stop outside. There is no visitor car park, but the harbour car park, four
minutes away on foot, has two hours of discounted parking with a museum ticket. Bicycle
racks are beside the main entrance.

## Cafe and shop
The Slack Water Cafe is on the ground floor of the West Wing and serves hot food until
15:30. The museum shop beside the main entrance stays open until the museum closes. Picnics
are not allowed in the galleries, but the courtyard has benches and visitors may eat there.
