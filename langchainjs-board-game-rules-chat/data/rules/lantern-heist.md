# Lantern Heist

2-4 players, 40 minutes. Players are thieves sneaking through the Gloamwick Observatory to steal glass lanterns before the night watch wakes.

## Setup
Shuffle the 24 Room tiles and lay out a 5x5 grid face down, leaving the centre open for the Watchman figure. Each player takes a thief figure, 3 Lockpick tokens and 1 Smoke Bomb token. Place the Watchman on the centre space.

## Turn order
On your turn you get 3 actions. You may move one space, peek at an adjacent face-down Room tile, pick a lock, or grab a lantern from the room you stand in. You may repeat the same action, but you may not grab more than one lantern per turn.

## The Watchman
After every player has taken a turn, flip a Watch card. The Watchman walks the number of spaces shown toward the nearest thief. If he ends his move on a thief, that thief is caught: drop all lanterns in that room and return to the entrance. A thief standing in a room with a spent Smoke Bomb token cannot be caught.

## Winning
The game ends when the 6th lantern leaves the observatory through the entrance. The thief with the most lanterns wins. Ties go to the thief with the most unspent Lockpick tokens.
