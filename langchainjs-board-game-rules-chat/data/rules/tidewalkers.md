# Tidewalkers

3-6 players, 60 minutes. Cooperative exploration: the tide is coming in and your crew has to cross the Saltglass Flats and reach the lighthouse together.

## Setup
Place the Flats board between the players and put the Tide marker on the first space of the Tide track. Each player picks a Tidewalker role card and takes 4 Supply cubes. Place the lighthouse token on the far end of the board.

## Turn order
Players act in any order they agree on, but each player must act once per round. Your action is one of: Wade (move up to 2 spaces), Salvage (take a Supply cube from your current space), Share (give a cube to a player on your space), or Brace (skip movement; you cannot be swept this round).

## The tide
At the end of each round, advance the Tide marker by 1. Every space at or below the Tide marker is flooded. A player standing on a flooded space who did not Brace is swept: move them back 3 spaces and discard 1 Supply cube. Players cannot Wade into a flooded space.

## Winning and losing
The crew wins if every player stands on the lighthouse space before the Tide marker reaches the last space on the track. If it does, or if any player has no cubes left to discard when swept, the crew loses together.
