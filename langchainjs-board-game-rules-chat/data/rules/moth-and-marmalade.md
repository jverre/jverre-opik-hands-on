# Moth & Marmalade

2-5 players, 25 minutes. A card game about running a tiny jam stall while moths raid your jars.

## Setup
Deal 5 Jar cards to each player. Put the Moth deck face down in the middle and flip the top three Moth cards to form the Swarm. The youngest player goes first.

## Turn order
Play proceeds clockwise. On your turn, draw 1 Jar card, then play up to 2 Jar cards in front of you as your stall. A stall may hold at most 6 jars; if you play a seventh, discard your oldest jar.

## Moths
At the end of your turn, count the jars in your stall whose flavour matches a Moth in the Swarm. Each matching jar is nibbled: turn it sideways. A sideways jar scores nothing. Discard the oldest Swarm Moth and flip a new one at the end of every full round.

## Trading
Once per turn you may swap one jar from your hand with a jar from a neighbour's hand, if they agree. You may never trade a sideways jar.

## Winning
When the Moth deck runs out, finish the round. Each upright jar scores its printed points. Highest total wins; ties are shared.
