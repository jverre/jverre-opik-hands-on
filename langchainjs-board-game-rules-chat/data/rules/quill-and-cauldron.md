# Quill & Cauldron

2-4 players, 35 minutes. A drafting game where apprentice witches write spells and brew them before the bell tolls.

## Setup
Give each player a Cauldron board and 3 Ink tokens. Shuffle the Spell deck and deal 4 cards to each player as their starting hand. Place the Bell marker on the first space of the Bell track.

## Turn order
Each round has two phases. In the Draft phase every player picks one card from their hand and passes the rest left; repeat until the hand is empty. In the Brew phase, starting with the player holding the Quill token, each player may brew any number of drafted spells by paying Ink equal to the spell's cost.

## Ink
You regain 1 Ink at the start of the Brew phase. You can never hold more than 5 Ink. Unbrewed spells stay in front of you and may be brewed in a later round, but you may keep at most 3 unbrewed spells; discard extras at the end of the Brew phase.

## The bell
After each round advance the Bell marker. When it reaches the last space, play one final round and the game ends. Scores are the printed points on brewed spells; the Quill holder breaks ties.
