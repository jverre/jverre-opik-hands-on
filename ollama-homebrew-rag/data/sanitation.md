# Copperkettle Handbook: Sanitation

## Clean first, then sanitize
Sanitizer cannot work through dirt. Every item is cleaned with a hot oxygen-based cleaner first and rinsed, and only then sanitized. Soak fermenters for 20 minutes in 50 C cleaner solution and scrub with a soft cloth; scratches harbor bacteria, so never use steel wool or a hard brush on plastic.

## Sanitizer contact time
Copperkettle uses an acid sanitizer mixed at 1 ml per litre of water. The contact time is 2 minutes; wet surfaces for less and they are not sanitized. Mixed solution is good for 24 hours as long as it stays clear and the pH is below 3. Foam is harmless and does not need rinsing.

## Bottling day
Bottles are cleaned in the dishwasher on the hottest cycle without detergent additives, then each bottle is filled with sanitizer solution, emptied, and left to drain upside down. Caps soak in sanitizer for 2 minutes. Prime with 6 g of table sugar per litre of beer for medium carbonation, and boil the sugar solution for 5 minutes before it touches the beer.

## Transfers and equipment
Anything that touches cooled wort or finished beer must be sanitized: the airlock, the thermometer probe, the hydrometer and its cylinder, tubing, and the sampling thief. Replace plastic tubing every 12 months because it develops tiny scratches. Keep a spray bottle of sanitizer next to the fermenter for your hands.
