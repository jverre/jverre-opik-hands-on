# City event permit notes (fictional)

- Road closure application must list every closed street and a marshal plan.
- One marshal per closure end, plus one roaming marshal per 400 attendees.
- Toilets: at least one unit per 150 expected attendees, and 10% accessible.
- Food vendors drawing over 40 amps must be placed on the Food row, which has the only heavy-duty power.
- Amplified music ends 20:30. Brass and acoustic acts are exempt but must finish by 21:00.
- A contingency reserve of at least 8% of the budget cap must be held back.
