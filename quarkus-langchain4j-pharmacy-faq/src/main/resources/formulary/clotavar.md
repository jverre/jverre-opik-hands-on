# Clotavar (fictional blood thinner)

## Dosage forms
Clotavar is prescription-only, supplied as 2.5 mg and 5 mg tablets in colour-coded blister cards: yellow for 2.5 mg, blue for 5 mg. Dose is set by the prescriber and recorded on the pharmacy label.

## Storage
Store at room temperature, protected from light. Keep the blister card inside the carton until the moment of use.

## Common interactions
Clotavar interacts with many products, including Tendrix, aspirin, and large changes in leafy-green vegetable intake. Never start or stop another medicine, supplement, or herbal product without asking the pharmacist.
