# Ferravel (fictional iron supplement)

## Dosage forms
Ferravel is a 65 mg iron tablet with a sugar coating, and a liquid drop formula for infants. The tablets are not chewable.

## Storage
Keep every Ferravel product locked away and out of reach of children, because iron overdose is dangerous for them. Store at room temperature in the original bottle with the desiccant packet.

## Common interactions
Take Ferravel apart from dairy products, tea, and coffee, which block absorption. Antacids and the acid reducer Lumidine also reduce how much iron is absorbed. Ferravel can darken stools; this is expected.
