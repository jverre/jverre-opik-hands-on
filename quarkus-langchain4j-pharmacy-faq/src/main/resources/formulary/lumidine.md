# Lumidine (fictional acid reducer)

## Dosage forms
Lumidine comes as 20 mg delayed-release capsules and as single-dose granule sachets. Capsules must be swallowed whole; the granule sachet is stirred into a spoonful of applesauce.

## Storage
Store capsules in the original blister pack below 25 C. Granule sachets must stay dry; do not keep them in a steamy kitchen cupboard above the kettle.

## Common interactions
Lumidine lowers stomach acid, which can reduce the absorption of the iron supplement Ferravel and the antifungal Mycostar. Separate these by at least two hours and ask the pharmacist about timing.
