# Calmorex (fictional antihistamine)

## Dosage forms
Calmorex is stocked as 10 mg tablets, a 5 mg/5 mL strawberry syrup, and 5 mg chewable tablets for children. Tablets are scored and may be halved. The syrup is dispensed with a marked oral syringe.

## Storage
Keep tablets and syrup at room temperature, away from the bathroom. Discard the syrup 28 days after the bottle is first opened, even if liquid remains.

## Common interactions
Calmorex adds to drowsiness caused by alcohol, sleeping aids, and other sedating antihistamines. It can be taken with food. Tell the pharmacist about any other allergy or cold product before combining.
