# Solvane (fictional pain reliever cream)

## Dosage forms
Solvane is a 4 percent topical cream in a 50 g tube and a roll-on gel in 75 mL bottles. It is for intact skin only.

## Storage
Store the tube upright with the cap tightly closed, below 30 C. The roll-on gel is flammable until dry, so keep it away from candles, cookers, and heaters.

## Common interactions
Do not apply heat pads or tight bandages over Solvane. People who already use Tendrix tablets should ask the pharmacist before using Solvane on large areas, because small amounts reach the bloodstream.
