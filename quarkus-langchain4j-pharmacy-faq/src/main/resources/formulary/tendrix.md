# Tendrix (fictional anti-inflammatory tablet)

## Dosage forms
Tendrix is available as 200 mg film-coated tablets in packs of 24 and 48, and as a 400 mg slow-release tablet that is sold from behind the counter.

## Storage
Keep in a cool dry place in the original pack. Do not transfer tablets to a weekly pill organizer for longer than seven days, because the coating absorbs moisture.

## Common interactions
Tendrix should be taken with food. It can interact with blood thinners such as Clotavar and with some blood-pressure medicines. Anyone taking Clotavar must speak to a pharmacist before buying Tendrix.
